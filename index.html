YOUR DATA

Privacy

A simple guide to what this app saves.

On this device

Your list, pantry, budget, and trip history are saved in this browser for offline use. When you join a group, the list and household shopping data also sync to Supabase.

Your account

Supabase stores your name and email to sign in. Household names, members, invite codes, and shared list items are stored in the app database. Only members of a household can read its shared data.

Item prices

You can save an item price and discount in your shopping list. These prices are estimates.

Your PIN

This device saves your email and a salted PIN check so you can open your list. It does not save the PIN itself.

Remove your data

Use Settings to clear this device. Use Delete account and data to remove your Supabase account and the household you own. Deleting a household also removes its shared list.
